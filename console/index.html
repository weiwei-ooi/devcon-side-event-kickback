Truth Pays · Owner Console

Every action opens your wallet for you to confirm. Keys never leave your wallet.

1. Connect wallet

Use the dedicated event wallet (the owner), not your personal one. The console switches the wallet to the right network.

2. Deploy the deposit contract (once)

Creates the contract that holds guest deposits. Your connected wallet becomes the owner. Costs a few cents of ETH.

Contract status

3. Register approved guests

Loads out/new-stakers.json: guests whose deposit Claude verified on-chain and whom you approved on Luma.

4. Refund declined guests

Loads out/refunds.json: paying guests you declined on Luma. Each gets exactly their deposit back to their own wallet, in one confirmation.

5. Post the attendance list after the event

Loads out/payout-plan.json (stakers who checked in). Publishes the list on-chain and opens a 1-day challenge window: a staker left off the list can challenge through Kleros. The button stays locked until the event starts, and you must type POST to confirm. Only possible once.

6. Challenges, then pay out

Anyone left off the list has 1 day to challenge (with an ETH deposit equal to the Kleros fee). Anyone, you included, then has 1 day to match that deposit if they disagree, which sends the case to Kleros jurors. A challenge nobody matches counts as attended. Payouts can be released once the window is over and no challenge is waiting for a match. A Kleros case does not hold the payout: everyone on the list is paid, and one share per open case is set aside until its final ruling (to the challenger if they win, otherwise split among the attendees).

Add someone you left off by mistake

During the 1-day window only. If they already challenged and nobody matched, their ETH deposit goes back to them.

Dispute or settle one challenge

Dispute = you believe this person did not attend: you match their ETH deposit and Kleros jurors decide (about 3 weeks; the challenger's share is set aside until the final ruling). Settle = close a challenge that was not matched in time, or whose Kleros ruling is in.

Appeal a Kleros ruling

After each jury vote there is an appeal window (a few days, shown in the challenge list). Anyone can chip in ETH for either answer. Fund appeal sends it from your wallet straight to Kleros (its appeal crowdfunding contract), never through the deposit contract, and Kleros records it under your wallet. The side that lost the vote must raise the appeal fee plus 2× and only has the first half of the window; the side that won raises the fee plus 1×. Only when both sides are fully funded does a new, larger jury vote again. After the final ruling, Collect appeal rewards takes back from Kleros what it owes this wallet: ETH back plus a reward for the side that won, or a refund for a side that was never fully funded (nothing for a funded side that lost). Uses the challenger wallet above.

Defend your side. If the other side funds an appeal and yours is not fully funded when the window closes, the other side wins the case, whatever the jurors voted. After every vote, watch the challenge list until the appeal window ends, and fund your side if the other side starts funding.

Send evidence to the Kleros jurors

For the challenger wallet above, while the case is open. Write what happened (door records, who saw them, photos). For a file, upload it first and paste its link (e.g. an IPFS link). Evidence is public and permanent.

Release payouts

Splits the whole pool equally between everyone on the final list. You must type PAY to confirm. Only possible once.

Other actions (only if needed)

Remove one guest and refund them

For a single registered staker you want to drop before the attendance list is posted. Sends exactly the deposit back to that wallet. For a payer who was never registered, decline them on Luma and use Refund declined guests (Claude's checked list) instead.

Return a stray transfer or overpayment

Only money that is not part of the staked pool can be returned this way (shown as “Spare” in the status). Before you confirm, the console lists what was already returned or refunded to that address, so the same money is not sent twice.

Collect leftover rounding dust

After the payout, a few millionths of a USDC of rounding may remain; this sends spare money to your wallet. Return guests' money first (declined, waitlisted and late payers, overpayments, stray transfers): it also counts as spare, and the console warns you if the spare is more than rounding dust. Unclaimed payouts always stay for their owners.

Cancel the event

Refunds every registered staker their deposit. Cannot be undone, and no payout is possible afterwards. Payers not registered yet are not included: afterwards, decline them on Luma and use Refund declined guests; unclaimed transfers go back with Return a stray transfer.
