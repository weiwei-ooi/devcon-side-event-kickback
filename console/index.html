Ground Truth · Owner Console

Every action opens your wallet for you to confirm. Keys never leave your wallet.

1. Connect wallet

Use the dedicated event wallet (the owner), not your personal one. The console switches the wallet to the right network.

2. Deploy the deposit contract (once)

Creates the contract that holds guest deposits. Your connected wallet becomes the owner. Costs a few cents of ETH.

Contract status

3. Register approved guests

Loads out/new-stakers.json: guests whose deposit Claude verified on-chain and whom you approved on Luma.

4. Refund declined guests

Loads out/refunds.json: paying guests you declined on Luma. Each gets exactly their deposit back to their own wallet, in one confirmation.

5. Pay out after the event

Loads out/payout-plan.json (stakers who checked in). Splits the whole pool equally between them. The button stays locked until the event starts, and you must type PAY to confirm. Only possible once.

Other actions (only if needed)

Remove one guest and refund them

For a single paying guest you want to decline or drop before the event. Sends exactly the deposit back to that wallet.

Return a stray transfer or overpayment

Only money that is not part of the staked pool can be returned this way (shown as “Spare” in the status).

Collect leftover rounding dust

After the payout, a few millionths of a USDC of rounding may remain; this sends spare money to your wallet. Refund declined, waitlisted and late payers first: their deposits also count as spare. Unclaimed payouts always stay for their owners.

Cancel the event

Refunds every registered staker their deposit. Cannot be undone, and no payout is possible afterwards.
