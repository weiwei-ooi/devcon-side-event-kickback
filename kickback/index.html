How it works

1

Book a spot

Guests send the deposit (ETH or the event's token) from their own wallet and are in at once. Booking stays open until the event ends, so walk-ins can book at the door.

2

Show up

After the event, the organiser posts who came. Anyone left off has a day to challenge it.

3

Get paid

The pool is split equally among everyone who came. No-shows lose their deposit. The organiser can never keep it.

Fair lists, settled by Kleros

Every event has the Kleros challenge built in. A guest left off the list can say "I was there". If anyone disagrees, the case goes to Kleros Court, where independent jurors follow a public attendance policy. No admin, no upgrades: nobody can change the rules or move deposits.
