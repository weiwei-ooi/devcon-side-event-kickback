Create your kickback event

Fill this in and confirm one transaction in your wallet. Your wallet becomes the organiser, and you get a link to share with guests.

Fixed for every event: after you close the attendance list, guests left off have 1 day to challenge it and anyone has 1 day to match a challenge, which opens a Kleros case. These settings cannot be changed after creation.

Costs only the network fee (about 400,000 gas). The organiser can give deposits back, post the list and pay everyone out, but can never keep deposits.
