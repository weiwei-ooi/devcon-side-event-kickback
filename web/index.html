Proof of Presence
OrganisersCode

Show up, get your deposit back, plus a share of the no-shows'.

Proof of Presence is an open protocol for event deposits. Guests book a spot with a small refundable deposit. After the event, everyone who came shares the whole pool. If the organiser leaves someone off the attendance list by mistake (or on purpose), that guest can challenge it, and Kleros Court decides.

1. Book a spot

Guests send the deposit (ETH or the event's token) from their own wallet. They are in at once. Deposits stay open until the event ends, so walk-ins can book on the spot.

2. Show up

After the event the organiser posts who came. Anyone left off has a challenge window (1 day) to say "I was there", and anyone can match the challenge within a day to send it to Kleros jurors.

3. Get paid

The pool is split equally among everyone on the final list. No-shows lose their deposit. Open Kleros cases never hold anyone else's money.

Why trust it?

  • Every event runs the same verified code, created by one factory with no owner. Nobody, not even the protocol's authors, can upgrade it or move deposits.
  • The organiser can only give deposits back (turn a guest away, cancel) or post the list. They cannot keep your deposit for themselves.
  • The list is checked by the people on it: a challenge goes to Kleros, a decentralised court whose jurors follow a public attendance policy.
  • If the organiser disappears, every guest can take their own deposit back after the deadline.
