Every action opens your wallet to confirm. Keys never leave your wallet.

1. Connect wallet

Use the event wallet (the owner), not your personal one. It is switched to Ethereum mainnet, the only network this event uses.

Connect the wallet you organise with. An event you create here is owned by it. You can look at an event without connecting.

2. Create a new event (once per event)

Creates your event's deposit contract on Ethereum mainnet, owned by your wallet. Nothing can be changed afterwards.

If no attendance list is posted in time, each guest can take their deposit back. Preview shows what Kleros jurors would read; it needs no wallet.

2. Deploy the deposit contract (once)

Creates the contract that holds the deposits. Your wallet becomes its owner.

Contract statusOpen an event

Paste the event's contract address. The deploy block is found for you when possible.

3. Register paid guests

A guest who paid is only paid out once registered here. Luma does not do it for you.

To pick by hand: tick Register or Refund, then press the button. Refund sends a guest exactly their deposit back.

Check the Luma guest list (optional)

On Luma: Manage → Guests → Export CSV, then pick the file. It checks each pasted tx link, puts names in the table and ticks Register or Refund. The file stays on this computer.

The Luma page needs:

  • A paid ticket with "deposit", "commit" or "stake" in its name, e.g. "Committed Spot (0.01 ETH)". No approval needed: a valid deposit is a spot.
  • A free ticket without those words. You can keep approval on for it.
  • The ETH address question, and a question named like "Deposit tx link (etherscan.io)".
  • After the event: the export's check-in column (checked_in_at) shows who came.
Use the scripts' lists instead (optional)

Uses out/new-stakers.json or out/refunds.json from Claude's check. Refused if anything changed since.

Files made by the command-line scripts. Refused if made for another contract or if anything changed since.

4. Post the attendance list after the event

Uses out/payout-plan.json (who checked in). Opens a 1-day challenge window. Locked until the event starts; type POST. Once only.

Export the Luma guests again after the event and pick the file (a door list is optional). Posting opens a 1-day challenge window. Locked until the event starts; type POST. Once only.

5. Challenges, then pay out

A guest left off the list has 1 day to challenge. Anyone then has 1 day to match it, which sends it to Kleros jurors. A challenge nobody matches counts as attended.

Add someone you left off by mistake

During the 1-day window only.

Dispute or settle one challenge

Dispute: you say they did not attend. You match their deposit and Kleros jurors decide (about 3 weeks). Settle: close a challenge nobody matched in time, or whose ruling is in. Publish case description: saves the case text jurors read, if the list says it is missing.

Appeal a Kleros ruling

After each jury vote there is an appeal window (see the list). Fund appeal sends ETH from your wallet straight to Kleros. After the final ruling, Collect appeal rewards takes back what Kleros owes this wallet. Use an ordinary wallet, not a Safe.

Defend your side. If only one answer is fully funded when the window closes, it wins, whatever the jurors voted. After every vote, watch the list and fund your side if the other side funds. "Refuse to arbitrate" funded alone also wins and counts as "did not attend". If you disputed, your side is "No, did not attend".

Send evidence to the Kleros jurors

For an open case. Attach photos and documents as files (up to 5 MB): jurors only see files stored on IPFS. Other links go in as text. Evidence is public and permanent.

Release payouts

Splits the pool equally among everyone on the final list. One share per open Kleros case waits for its ruling. Type PAY. Once only.

Other actions (only if needed)

Remove one guest and refund them

For one registered guest, before the list is posted. For a payer not registered, use Refund selected in step 3.

Return a stray transfer or overpayment

Only spare ETH (“Spare” in the status), never the staked pool. Earlier returns to that address are shown first.

Return tokens sent by mistake

Tokens (USDC, for example) are never deposits: send them back here. This never moves ETH.

Collect leftover rounding dust

After the payout. Return guests' money first: you are warned if the spare is more than rounding dust.

Cancel the event

Refunds every registered guest. Cannot be undone. Payers not registered: refund them with Refund selected afterwards.
