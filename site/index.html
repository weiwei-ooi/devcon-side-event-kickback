      Missed at check-in? Challenge it on Kleros

      After the event the hosts publish the attendance list on-chain. If you were there but are not on it, you have 1 day to challenge from this page with a small ETH deposit (the Kleros arbitration fee, about 0.015 ETH). Anyone who disagrees has 1 day to match it. If nobody does, you count as attended and get your ETH back. If someone does, independent Kleros jurors decide from the evidence (photos, messages, anything that shows you were there), and the winner gets the remaining ETH. Either side can appeal a jury's vote: the case then goes to a larger jury, and anyone can help fund the appeal from this page. If one side funds an appeal and the other doesn't, the funded side wins, so keep an eye on your case until the final ruling. A Kleros case doesn't hold up everyone's payout: guests on the list are paid, and the challenger's share is set aside until the final ruling.

      The deposit address shown here is the same one on the Luma event page. Hosts never ask for your recovery phrase or private key. The contract can't pay anything out before the event starts, a guest left off the attendance list can challenge it on Kleros, and if the hosts never settle, every confirmed guest can withdraw their own deposit after the claim deadline.
