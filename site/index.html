      Missed at check-in? Challenge it on Kleros

      After the event the hosts publish the attendance list on-chain. If you were there but are not on it, you have 1 day to challenge from this page with a small ETH deposit (the Kleros arbitration fee, about 0.016 ETH). Anyone who disagrees has 1 day to match it. If nobody does, you count as attended and get your ETH back. If someone does, independent Kleros jurors decide from the evidence (photos, messages, anything that shows you were there), and the winner gets the remaining ETH. A round takes about 3 weeks. Either side can appeal a jury's vote: the case then goes to a larger jury, and anyone can help fund the appeal from this page. Appeal money goes straight from your wallet to Kleros, and Kleros pays any refund or reward back to that wallet. If one side funds an appeal and the other doesn't, the funded side wins, so keep an eye on your case until the final ruling. A Kleros case doesn't hold up everyone's payout: once the challenge window is over, anyone can trigger the payout, guests on the list are paid, and the challenger's share is set aside until the final ruling.
